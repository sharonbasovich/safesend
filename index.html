End-to-end walkthrough

In this two-minute captioned demo, a poisoned history entry triggers a lookalike warning and on-chain quarantine. The recipient cannot claim after the lock without sender approval. An approved claim then verifies that exact address for future instant sends.

Recorded on a fresh local Anvil chain with synthetic addresses and mock mUSDT. The earlier cancellation walkthrough shows a sender recovering escrowed funds; the current contract also permits cancellation after approval while the escrow is pending. No mainnet funds or paid keys were used.

One router, three send paths

01 · Verified

Instant delivery

Known payees receive funds directly. No escrow or waiting period.

02 · Unknown

Cancellable escrow

New recipients claim after the sender's cooldown. The sender can cancel while pending.

03 · Lookalike

Lock and approval

A first/last-four fingerprint collision raises an on-chain flag and a 24-hour lock. The recipient can claim only after the sender approves that escrow. A successful claim verifies the full address for future instant sends.

Run the real app locally

The contracts and interactive web UI are open source. You need Foundry and Node.js; the README has the complete setup.

git clone https://github.com/sharonbasovich/safesend.git
cd safesend
make anvil          # separate terminal
make deploy-local
make seed
make web            # http://localhost:5173/?demo=1
